# Research Hypotheses — AnyCompany Insights Platform

**Phase:** 1 → feeds Phase 2 (Ideation)
Derived from `signals/signal-summary.md`. Each hypothesis is testable in Phase 4
(Validation).

> Fictional example — synthetic sample feedback, no real customer.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 — Guided onboarding reduces early abandonment.** Adding in-product guided
  onboarding and refreshed docs will raise first-run setup completion and lower
  early churn. (from `review_001`)
- **H2 — Role-based simplification drives team adoption.** Giving non-technical
  roles a simplified view will stop teams reverting to spreadsheets. (from
  `review_003`)
- **H3 — Reliable reporting retains power users.** Fixing export dates, chart
  rendering, and persistent filters removes a top frustration for
  reporting-heavy users. (from `review_004`)
- **H4 — An SMB pricing tier expands the funnel.** A startup / low-volume tier
  converts price-sensitive small teams currently priced out. (from `review_006`)
- **H5 — Accurate multilingual support unlocks non-English segments.** Better
  translation and non-English sentiment analysis serves customers writing in
  German/French. (from `review_008`, `review_009`)

## Do-Not-Break (validated strengths)

AI auto-categorization, mobile app + push notifications, Slack integration +
sentiment alerts, and the developer API/webhooks are working well — retain and
build on these rather than disrupt them.
